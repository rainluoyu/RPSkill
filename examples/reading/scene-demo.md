The observation-room window held the last of the evening light. Bob turned the tide lamp halfway around. Tiny brass plates inside its base struck one another with a faint, almost complaining click.

Alice stopped in the doorway and asked why he had not rested yet.

“It's three beats behind the clock tower.” Bob left the adjustment screw alone and moved the record sheet closer to the lamp. “This afternoon it was two.”

Outside, a young messenger searched beneath the eaves for somewhere dry. He knocked on the open door and handed Bob a paper bag that the rain had not reached.

“From the harbor. They said not to let the lamp go out.”

Bob took the bag, his fingers pausing at the seal. He looked toward the doorway and waited for Alice's reply. The tide lamp continued to brighten and dim over two unfinished record sheets.
