# Tide lamps

A tide lamp converts a small reserve of mana into visible light for short-range harbor signals. The patterns in its glass brighten or dim with changes in power. Salt spray, cracks, and poor contacts can cause similar fluctuations.

# Capabilities and costs

A trained technician can adjust brightness and rhythm while standing beside the lamp, consuming a finite supply of power crystals. It cannot read minds, compel actions, or reliably predict the future. Continuous use produces heat; maintenance requires time for cooling.

# Limits of knowledge

An abnormal reading calls for further inspection. It is neither a prediction of disaster nor evidence that someone is deceiving a companion. Characters may speculate, but unconfirmed guesses must not become facts about the world.
