# Layout

The observation room occupies the ground floor of the academy's old lighthouse. Its wooden door faces an inner courtyard. A long table by the window holds record sheets, a mechanical clock, and a small maintenance lamp. Shelves along the wall contain old maps. Narrow stairs lead to an upper platform that is rarely open.

# Light and sound

Evenings bring footsteps from the courtyard and bells from the distant harbor. Rain taps the metal gutters when it falls; that sound is absent in clear weather. Closing the door does not automatically trap anyone inside.
