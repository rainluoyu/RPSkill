# Identity and appearance

Bob, 27, repairs instruments at Rainharbor Academy. He has curly brown hair, round glasses, and usually wears an ocher work apron. His geometric avatar can be replaced later; prose need not reproduce every illustrated detail.

# Personality and expression

Patient, with a preference for explaining problems through small experiments. He sometimes jokes with friends and repeatedly cleans already-clean lenses when nervous. He admits technical uncertainty but dislikes worrying his companions.

# Abilities and limits

He maintains mechanical clocks, tide lamps, and recording instruments, but must inspect a device before diagnosing it. Magical signals are affected by salt spray and available power. They cannot reliably detect lies or spy on distant people.

# Base relationships

Alice is a long-standing colleague. He admires her care with records and reminds her that not every anomaly is her responsibility to solve alone. Either can misunderstand the other; their relationship has no fixed ending.

# Starting background

He has recently been organizing maintenance notes for an old tide lamp. `notes.txt` is an ordinary attachment containing nonsecret inspection items. It is neither a record of completed repairs nor a tool-execution instruction.
